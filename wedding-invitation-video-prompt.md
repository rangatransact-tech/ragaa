## 1. Global World Bible (paste this before every scene prompt)

> **World:** A grand South Indian Dravidian temple complex (like Annavaram, Madurai or Srirangam). It has a tall, intricately carved stone *gopuram* (tower gateway), long pillared *mandapams* (halls) with sculpted granite columns, stone-flagged courtyards and brass oil lamps (*samai/kuthuvilakku*) everywhere.
>
> **Style:** Hyper-detailed 3D animated illustration with a semi-realistic Pixar/Disney-style feel for the characters and photoreal stone, brass and flowers. Cinematic, warm and devotional.
>
> **Light:** Golden hour throughout (low sun behind the gopuram, volumetric god-rays, lens flare and bloom), except the reception scene, which is at blue-hour dusk lit by fairy lights.
>
> **Palette:** Saffron, marigold orange, turmeric yellow, jasmine white, antique gold, warm sandstone and deep maroon.
>
> **Recurring set dressing:** Thick marigold and jasmine garlands (*thoranams*) draped on every pillar and arch, lit clay diyas lining the floors, flower petals scattered on stone, slowly drifting incense smoke, hanging brass bells, and kolam/rangoli patterns with flower petals.
>
> **Ambient motion in every shot:** Petals (marigold, rose, jasmine) drift down slowly. Diya flames flicker. Incense smoke curls upward. Dust motes float in the sunbeams. Garlands sway gently.
>
> **Typography:** Elegant gold calligraphic script for names. A serif engraved in gold for details. All text sits on ornate gold-framed boards, easels or scrolls placed *inside* the 3D world, and is never a flat overlay.
>
> **Camera:** Smooth, slow, gimbal-like moves only: dolly-in, dolly-out, crane and orbit. No handheld shake. Every transition is a continuous camera move through a pillar, doorway or arch (a "pass-through wipe"), or a light-bloom dissolve.
>
> **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 24 fps, 4K.
>
> **Audio:** Soft nadaswaram/veena instrumental with temple bells and light mridangam.

## 2. Scene-by-Scene Shot List

### Scene 1: Temple Establishing Shot (0:00–0:03)
- **Frame:** High aerial view at sunrise. The towering gopuram stands center frame with the sun rising just behind its top tiers. Coconut palms surround the complex and morning mist lies over the fields. A smaller shrine (*vimana*) sits in the foreground courtyard. Smoke rises from small fire pits.
- **Motion:** The drone descends slowly and pushes forward along the central axis toward the gopuram. Sun flare pulses. Birds glide across the sky in the distance.
- **Transition:** The camera keeps flying forward into a shadowed stone doorway, and the frame goes dark-gold.

### Scene 2: Ganesha Blessing (0:03–0:08)
- **Frame:** Inside a pillared mandapam, looking out through a tall doorway at the sunlit gopuram. A seated, colorful **Lord Ganesha idol** (gold, red and pink) rests on a stone altar. The altar is wrapped in marigold and jasmine garlands, with tall brass lamps on both sides. Brass bells hang from the lintel.
- **Text:** "**Om Shree Ganeshaya Namah**" in glowing gold serif, carved into the lintel above the doorway. It fades in with a shimmer that sweeps left to right.
- **Motion:** A bright sunburst pops from the top of the frame. The camera does a slow dolly-in toward the idol. Petals start falling from above and the bells sway gently. Lamp flames brighten.
- **Transition:** The camera drifts right past a carved granite pillar, which fills the frame as a wipe (0:07–0:08).

### Scene 3: Invitation Title Scroll (0:08–0:12)
- **Frame:** An open-air courtyard with the gopuram in the background and side mandapams. A cream-and-gold **framed scroll board** stands on a garlanded stone pedestal in the center.
- **Text:** "**[FAMILY NAME]'s Wedding Invitation**" in gold calligraphy with small ornament flourishes above and below.
- **Motion:** The camera holds briefly, then does a slow **dolly-out**, pulling back under a scalloped stone arch that frames the shot. Petals fall and diyas on the floor flicker.
- **Transition:** A gold glow blooms and dissolves into a dark doorway.

### Scene 4: Groom Name Reveal (0:12–0:17)
- **Frame:** A long pillared corridor with sculpted columns and garlands looking down a deep perspective. Floor diyas line the path.
- **Text animation:** At 0:12 the groom's name is written on in **glowing, light-painted calligraphy**, stroke by stroke, inside a dark doorway. Sparkles trail the pen tip. The text then settles as "*Chi.* **[GROOM NAME]**" in gold script, offset to the right.
- **Character:** The **groom** appears on the left, standing barefoot. He is a stylized 3D character wearing a cream silk *pancha/veshti* with a gold border, an angavastram over one shoulder, and a gentle smile.
- **Motion:** He blinks, smiles softly and breathes subtly. The camera moves in very slowly. Petals fall and incense smoke rises beside him.
- **Transition:** A camera move to the right past a garlanded pillar into the next courtyard.

### Scene 5: Bride Name Reveal (0:17–0:21)
- **Frame:** A courtyard viewed between two garlanded pillars, with the gopuram glowing in the background. Marigold bunting hangs across the far hall and a row of diyas lines a stone step.
- **Text:** "*Chi. Lau. Sow.* **[BRIDE NAME]**" in gold script. It first floats in mid-air, center-right (0:17), then settles beside the bride.
- **Character:** The **bride** stands on the left in a maroon-and-gold Kanjeevaram silk saree with jasmine in her hair (*poolajada*) and gold temple jewelry. She has a shy smile and her hands are folded in front.
- **Motion:** She tilts her head slightly and smiles. Her saree pallu flutters a little. The camera holds, then pushes right toward the pillar.
- **Transition:** The pillar wipes the frame.

### Scene 6: Bride's Parentage (0:21–0:26)
- **Frame:** A low three-quarter angle of an ivory board with a gold filigree frame, leaning on a carved wooden stand. Garlands drape around it, a pillar sits beside it, and the temple vimana shows in the soft-focus background.
- **Text:** "**[BRIDE NAME]** / Second Daughter of / **Sri [FATHER NAME] & Smt. [MOTHER NAME]**" with a small gold gopuram emblem at the top.
- **Motion:** A slow orbit and push-in from the left (the board grows from 60% to 90% of the frame). The camera ends tilted dramatically, with the pillar on the right.
- **Note:** Add a matching "Groom: Son of…" board if you want both sets of parents.

### Scene 7: Sumuhurtham Ceremony (0:26–0:30)
- **Frame:** A wedding mandapam (*kalyana vedika*) heavy with garlands. The couple sits cross-legged on a red rug before the sacred fire (*homam*). The groom places **jeelakarra-bellam** (cumin-jaggery) on the bride's head. Plates of fruit and flowers surround them, with guests blurred in the background. A maroon board in a gold frame stands on the right.
- **Text:** "**Sumuhurtham:** On [DAY] [DATE] at [TIME] hrs ([NOTE])"
- **Motion:** The homam fire flickers and smoke rises. The groom's hand rests on the bride's head and she lowers her eyes and smiles. The camera tracks slowly left to right.
- **Transition:** A whip-pan to the right through a brass lamp pole, which acts as a vertical wipe.

### Scene 8: Venue (0:30–0:35)
- **Frame:** A wide temple courtyard in front of the gopuram. A large maroon-and-gold board stands on a pedestal ringed by flower rangoli and brass lamps. Guests in sarees and veshtis stand on both sides in prayer pose.
- **Text:** "**Venue:** [VENUE NAME, TEMPLE, TOWN]"
- **Motion:** The shot starts from behind a pillar on the left and trucks right to reveal the board. Guests sway subtly and incense drifts. The camera then pulls back under a garlanded arch.
- **Transition:** The light shifts warm-to-blue as the camera crosses a curtain (day turns to dusk).

### Scene 9: Reception (0:35–0:40)
- **Frame:** Blue-hour night sky. A decorated reception arch with marigold, white and maroon swags and golden fairy-light curtains. A gilded easel holds a cream board, and a crowd of guests mingles behind it.
- **Text:** "**Reception – Dinner:** On [DAY] [DATE], From [TIME] onwards at [VENUE, ADDRESS]"
- **Motion:** Start close on the easel from the side, then dolly back to a symmetrical wide shot. Lots of petals and sparkles fall like confetti and the fairy lights twinkle.
- **Transition:** A slow drift to the left past a dark carved pillar.

### Scene 10: Invited By (0:40–0:46)
- **Frame:** An ornate inner sanctum (*garbhagriha*) entrance with a symmetrical composition. A golden board sits under layered garland swags with rows of brass lamps on both sides and a flower rangoli on the floor.
- **Text:** "**Invited By:** [NAMES OF HOSTS]"
- **Motion:** A static hold for 4 s with flickering lamps, then a **long dolly-out** that reveals the full hall corridor and floor reflections.
- **Transition:** A quick light-flash cut to a close-up.

### Scene 11: Couple Close-up (0:46–0:47)
- **Frame:** A tight two-shot of the bride and groom wearing wedding garlands (*varamala*). They laugh and look at each other, with a shallow depth of field and bokeh lamps behind them.
- **Motion:** A slight handheld-style breath. Both smile broadly.

### Scene 12: Save the Date Finale (0:47–0:55)
- **Frame:** A grand processional path leading to the gopuram, with a flower arch in the background. The couple stands center-left wearing wedding garlands (bride in a maroon silk saree, groom in white and gold). A rich brown-and-gold easel stands on the right. Strings of marigold garlands hang from the top of the frame, homa fires smoke on the side, and diyas and marigold borders line the path.
- **Text:** "**Save the Date** / [DATE 1], [DATE 2]"
- **Motion:** The couple turns toward each other, the bride leans her head toward the groom and they share a tender forehead-touch. Meanwhile the camera **pulls back slowly** through a carved stone archway, and a hanging brass lamp descends into the top of the frame. Petals keep falling.
- **End:** Hold the final frame for 2 s, then fade to warm black.

## 3. How to Produce It

Claude can't render video itself. Use Claude to write and refine the prompts, then generate the clips in a video model such as **Veo 3, Kling, Sora, Runway Gen-4, Hailuo or Pika**:

1. **Make the stills first** (Midjourney, Imagen, ChatGPT image or Flux) for each scene using *World Bible + scene "Frame" + "Text"*. Put the text on the boards here, because image models spell better than video models. Alternatively, leave the boards blank and add the text later in CapCut or Canva.
2. **Lock your characters:** create one bride reference image and one groom reference image (you can upload your own photos and ask for a "stylized 3D Pixar-style version"). Reuse them as character references in every scene.
3. **Animate each still** with image-to-video using the scene's "Motion" lines. Keep clips at 4–6 s.
4. **Edit** in CapCut or Premiere: put the clips in order, add the pillar-wipe and light-flash transitions, music and a gentle petal overlay.
